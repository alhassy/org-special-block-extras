* TODO Rename =lisp/= to =src/=, split into =core/= and =derived/=

Every =.el= file sits in =lisp/=, which MELPA's default recipe ships. We rename it =src/=:
=core/= holds the building blocks, and =derived/= holds strictly the block definitions
and the helpers they use.

#+begin_example
src/
├── org-special-block-extras.el      ; the entry point, requires all of derived/
├── tooltips.css, tooltips.js        ; beside the file that inlines them
├── core/
│   ├── org-defblock.el
│   ├── org-defblock-tag.el
│   ├── org-special-block-parse.el
│   ├── org-block-{switches,export,fontify}.el
│   ├── org-deflink.el
│   ├── org-deftag.el
│   ├── org-thread-blockcall.el
│   ├── org-block-header-args.el
│   ├── org-special-block-at-point.el
│   ├── org-special-block-indirect-edit.el
│   ├── org-special-block-split.el
│   └── org-ctrl-c-ctrl-c-for-special-block.el
└── derived/
    ├── org-{align,box,demo,details,math,parallel,
    │        proof,quote,remark,solution,spoiler,style}-block.el
    ├── org-{callout,margin,substitution}-blocks.el
    └── graph/                       ; today's org-graph-*.el
#+end_example
